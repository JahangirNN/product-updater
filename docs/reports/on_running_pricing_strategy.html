3. Three Formula Options: Pick the One That Fits Your Goals

We developed and simulated three distinct pricing formulas for leadership to choose from:

Option A: The Market Penetration Formula (Aggressive Undercut) Fast Growth / Lowest Price
Selling Price = ROUND(Source_USD × 88 × 1.18 + 300, -2) - 1
How it works: Applies an 18% markup over landed cost plus a ₹300 order buffer, ending in ₹...99.
Price Examples: $160 shoe sells for ₹16,899 (Net profit: ₹2,899 | 16.7% margin). $180 shoe sells for ₹18,999 (Net profit: ₹3,249).
Verdict: Guaranteed to be the cheapest price in India on almost every model, but leaves money on the table since buyers are already willing to pay ₹17.5k+.
Option B: The Strategic "Price Attack" Formula (RECOMMENDED) ★ RECOMMENDED ★
Selling Price = ROUND((Source_USD × Live_FX) + 2,350 + (Source_USD - 160) × 10, -2) - 1
How it works: Designed specifically for our system architecture. At base $160, it adds ₹2,350 over system forex, smoothly scaling up by ₹10 for every additional dollar. Automatically ends in ₹...99.
Price Examples:
  • $120 Entry Shoe: Sells at ₹13,399 (Beats CDC ₹13,499 & Hypefly ₹14,199). Profit: ₹2,839.
